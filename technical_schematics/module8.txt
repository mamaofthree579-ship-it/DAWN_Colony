
🔩 Technical Specification Sheet: ROB-08


Module 8 — Automated Mining & Sintering Rover Depot (ROB-08)

Revision: A
Classification: Autonomous Heavy Industrial Robotics Platform
Primary Function: Continuous resource extraction, slurry material transport, and in-situ multi-axis printing/sintering.

1.0 Functional Description

The Automated Mining & Sintering Rover Depot (ROB-08) serves as the primary physical logistics vehicle network for the Dawn Colony. It manages:
• Strip-mining of surface regolith down to 1.5 meters depth.
• Sorting and mechanical screening of raw geological matrices (separating coarse basalt aggregates from high-silica dust).
• Mixing and hydration of the geopolymer composite slurry using recycled waste water or raw subsurface meltwater streams.
• Autonomous multi-axis robotic-arm extrusion pathing to construct modules in-situ without manual scaffolding.

2.0 Subsystems & Fleet Composition

The depot consists of three standardized autonomous vehicle platforms communicating via the PCS-07 Phased Array Microwave Mesh Network:

2.1 Fleet Vector A: Excavator-Hauler Node (EHN-01)

• Mass: \(11,500\text{ kg}\) (Martian Working Gravity Weight Equivalent: \(4,370\text{ kg}\)).
• Drive Configuration: 6-wheel high-clearance titanium mesh wheels with integrated in-wheel brushless magnetic drives.
• Payload Capacity: \(8.5\text{ m}^3\) raw regolith capacity per cycle.

2.2 Fleet Vector B: Mobile Sintering & Printing Unit (MPU-02)

• Arm Geometry: 6-Axis industrial articulation system with a \(6.2\text{ m}\) total reach envelope.
• Print Head Assembly: Outfitted with the calibrated 80 mm auger extruder driven by high-torque closed-loop stepper motors.
• Thermal Core: Integrated \(10.6\ \mu\text{m}\) \(\text{CO}_{2}\) laser optics array (continuous 4.5 kW thermal output) for precision sintering of geopolymer margins.

3.0 Operational Metrics

• Continuous Travel Power Draw: 8.5 kW nominal.
• Peak Layer Deposition Draw (Auger Extrusion + Laser Sintering active): 28 kW.
• Onboard Energy Bank: \(150\text{ kWh}\) Lithium-Glass solid-state battery array paired with top-mounted self-cleaning solar collection wings.
• Navigational Autonomy System: Dual-frequency LiDAR path mapping matched with an real-time edge-computing navigation deck to process toolpaths during localized communications blackouts.
